# Birthday spot-check

Every dated founder was checked by a reviewer agent against Wikipedia and Wikidata, and every proposed change was re-checked by a second, skeptical agent before it was applied (research/birthday_checks.json holds all verdicts).

Totals: 325 confirmed · 31 wrong (namesakes or unsupported days; retracted or corrected) · 13 found for previously undated founders · 29 unsure (day rests on an uncited Wikidata value; kept, flagged) · 148 still missing.
